## 1. Headline

- **PLRU works, and it is the biggest single gain so far — for the plain L2.** On the board (64 KB, omnetpp,
  fixed work) PLRU instead of random replacement: **−3.56% cycles, −12.65% main-memory traffic, +3.61 points
  hit rate**, with SBC's migration switched off.
- **SBC still does not beat the plain L2.** With PLRU on in both halves: **+0.07% cycles, +0.41% memory
  traffic**. With random in both halves: +0.07% cycles, −0.02% traffic. A tie that leans the wrong way.
- **Migrations pay back twice as much, but still under break-even:** secondary hits per migration
  0.22 (random) → **0.44** (PLRU). Break-even is about 1. Half of all guests are evicted clean, unused.
- **Teardown finally works** (B7-1 fix): second searches on the 128 KB board fell from 7.94% of accesses
  (0.03% hit) to 0.054% (8.16% hit).
